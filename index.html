Questions

Before you order

Quick answers to what clients ask most.

How does ordering work?

Pick a package, fill in the form below and send your brief on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm the details and your start date.

How do I pay?

We start once 50% is paid. The other 50% is paid when your design is ready, and you receive the final files after that. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp.

How long does it take?

Brand Starter: first concepts in 48 hours and final files in 5 days. Growth & Social Pack: 10 to 15 days. Elite Brand Suite: 21 days. Days count from when we receive your payment and full details.

How many revisions do I get?

Brand Starter includes 2 rounds. Growth & Social Pack and Elite Brand Suite include 3. A round is one set of changes sent together. Extra rounds are quoted on WhatsApp.

What files will I receive?

PNG and JPG files. Logos come in transparent and white-background versions. Vector files (SVG) are not included.

Do I own the design?

Yes. Full copyright is handed over to you once the final payment is made.

Do you take custom projects or clients outside Nigeria?

Yes to both. Choose "Not sure yet" in the form, or message us on WhatsApp, and we will send a custom quote or a dollar price.
